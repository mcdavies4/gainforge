# GainForge — Product brief

## Vision
A global AI growth workspace that helps businesses find relevant opportunities, decide what to do next, execute with human oversight, and measure real outcomes.

## Core promise
Find the next best growth opportunity—and help turn it into a customer.

## Initial user
A founder or small-business owner who needs more customers but cannot justify a full growth team.

## MVP workflow
1. Business enters website, audience, region and growth goal.
2. System creates a structured business profile.
3. Opportunity engine finds evidence-backed opportunities from approved sources.
4. Each result includes source, date, evidence, reason for relevance, confidence and next action.
5. User approves or edits the action.
6. User tracks replies, qualified leads and conversions.
7. Where data is connected, product attributes revenue; otherwise it clearly says unmeasured.

## Product principles
- Evidence before automation.
- Human approval before external outreach.
- Never present an AI estimate as a verified lead or actual revenue.
- Respect source terms, privacy, anti-spam laws and platform rules.
- Focus on outcomes customers will pay for, not vanity metrics.

## MVP not included yet
Authentication, database persistence, real web crawling, live data integrations, AI API, billing, team access, automatic sending, and real analytics.
